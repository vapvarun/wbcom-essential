# Shop blocks and widgets (WooCommerce integration)

**Also called:** product grid, product slider, product tabs, sale banner, customer reviews, store page, online shop, WooCommerce blocks.

## What this does

This page lists the 3 blocks and 5 Elementor widgets that show your shop: products, reviews and sale banners. They work with WooCommerce 7.0 or later.

## Before you start

1. Install and activate WooCommerce.
2. Add products and, for reviews, collect some product reviews.

If WooCommerce is inactive, a WooCommerce block tells you that WooCommerce is required.


## Steps

Add a block from the **Essential - WooCommerce** category, or an Elementor widget from the **Wbcom Essential** category.

### Blocks

| Block | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| Product Grid | Products in a responsive grid with category filter, sale badge, star rating and add-to-cart button |
| Product Carousel | Products in a carousel with autoplay, arrows and dots |
| Customer Reviews | Product reviews in a carousel with avatar, star rating, reviewer name and product link |

Details: [WooCommerce blocks](../blocks/woocommerce-blocks.md).

### Elementor widgets

All widgets are in the **Wbcom Essential** category and appear only when WooCommerce is active.

| Widget | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Universal Product | Product display |
| Product Tab | Products in tabs |
| Add Banner | Promotional banner |
| Customer Review | Product reviews |
| WooCommerce Testimonial | Testimonials from WooCommerce |

Details: [Shop widgets](../widgets/woocommerce-widgets.md).

## You'll know it worked when

The Product Grid block shows your products instead of a notice.

## Good to know

### What page ideas can I use?


- **Shop homepage:** Hero Section, Product Carousel, Promo Banner, Product Grid, Customer Reviews, CTA Section.
- **Sale page:** Countdown Timer above a Product Grid filtered to a sale category.

See [Build a shop home page](online-store-tutorial.md).

### Can I sell digital downloads?


Use Easy Digital Downloads and the EDD blocks instead. See [EDD blocks](../blocks/edd-blocks.md).
