# WooCommerce Widgets

The 5 WooCommerce widgets show products, banners and customer reviews. They appear in the Elementor panel under the **Wbcom Essential** category.

Every widget in this group needs WooCommerce. If WooCommerce is not active, the widgets do not appear in the panel.

## Add Banner

A promotional banner with an image, title, sub title and button.

### Content settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Banner > Style | Chooses the banner layout style. |
| Banner > Content Alignment | Aligns the banner text. |
| Banner > Banner image | The banner image. |
| Banner > Banner Title, Banner Sub Title | The banner text. |
| Banner > Button Text, Button Link | The button label and its link. |

### Style settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Title | Colour, typography and margin. |
| Sub Title | Colour, typography and margin. |
| Button | Colour, hover colour and typography. |

## Customer Review

A grid of customer reviews with name, designation, rating and photo.

### Content settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Review > Style | Chooses the review layout style. |
| Review > Review Type | Chooses the type of review item. |
| Review > Name, Designation, Client Rating, Image, Client Say | The review content. |
| Option | Columns, No Gutters, Space and Bottom Space. |

### Style settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Area | Alignment, padding, background, border and radius. |
| Image | Border and radius. |
| Name, Designation, Client say | Colour, margin and padding. |
| Rating | Colour. |

## Product Tab

Products grouped under tabs, or shown as a slider.

### Content settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Product Settings | Product Style, Filter By, Columns, Rows, Number of Products, Product Categories, Custom Order with Order by and Order, and the Product Tab and Product Slider switches. |
| Tab Menu Style | Alignment, plus colour, tab menu background, border and radius in Normal and Hover states, and tab menu padding and margin. |
| Slider Option | Slider Items, Slider Arrow, Slider dots, Pause on Hover, Slider autoplay with speeds, and Slider item to scroll, plus separate Tablet and Mobile Phone items and resolutions. |

### Style settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Slider Controller Style | Colours, background, border, radius and padding for the navigation arrows and dots in Normal and Hover states. |

## Universal Product

A flexible product grid or slider with query, badge and action button options.

### Content settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Layout Settings | Layout, Same Height Box and Columns. |
| Query Settings | Filter By, Select Product, Product IDs, Product Limit, Product Categories, Exclude Out Of Stock Item, Exclude Hidden Item, Custom Order, Order by and Order. |
| Content Settings | Style, Title HTML Tag, and switches to hide the title, price, category, category before border and rating. |
| Action Button Settings | Action Button, Style, Show on, Position and Show Add to Cart Button Text. |
| Image Settings | Thumbnails Style, and the arrow, dot and border colours. |
| Slider Option | Slider Items, Slider Arrow, Slider dots, Pause on Hover, Slider autoplay with speeds, and Slider item to scroll, plus separate Tablet and Mobile Phone items and resolutions. |

### Style settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Style | Padding, margin, border colour and hover box shadow of each product box. |
| Content area | Padding, background colour and border. |
| Product Badge | Badge colour, out of stock badge colour, badge background colour and padding. |
| Product Category | Typography, colour, hover colour and margin. |
| Product Title | Typography, colour and hover colour. |

## WooCommerce Testimonial

Customer testimonials as a grid or a slider.

### Content settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Testimonial > Style, Review Type | Chooses the layout style and review type. |
| Testimonial > Name, Designation, Client Rating, Image, Client Say | The testimonial content. |
| Slider On | Turns the slider on. When it is off, Columns, No Gutters, Space and Bottom Space control the grid. |
| Slider Option | Slider Items, Slider Arrow, Slider dots, Pause on Hover, Slider autoplay with speeds, and Slider item to scroll, plus separate Tablet and Mobile Phone items and resolutions. |

### Style settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Item | Border, radius, padding and margin. |
| Image | Border, radius and image area border colour. |
| Name, Designation, Client say | Colour, margin and padding. |
| Rating | Colour. |
