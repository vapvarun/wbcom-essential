## Triage

ajax-nopriv (1): `wbcom_ajax_login` in plugins/elementor/wbcom-essential-elementor.php, unchanged since 4.7.2. It is the Login Form widget, so it serves logged-out visitors by definition. It checks the form nonce (CSRF), locks out after repeated failures (QA verified: fourth attempt gives "Too many login attempts. Try again in 15 minutes."), and gives the same message for an unknown user and a wrong password. Intended.

no-abspath-guard 6 -> 8: the new file is phpstan-edd-stubs.php, a PHPStan-only stub that the gruntfile, .distignore and build-release.sh all keep out of the zip. Nothing new ships without a guard.

Surface: 6 REST routes, 6 permission callbacks, unchanged. nonce-only-auth and extract-call counts unchanged from 4.7.2.

Verdict: no G1 CRITICAL that is not intended, no new HIGH. Pass.
